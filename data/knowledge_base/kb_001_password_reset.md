title: Resetting your ContosoCloud password
category: account
---

If you've forgotten your password or need to reset it for security reasons, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to https://accounts.contosocloud.com/forgot
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email (it should arrive within 5 minutes)
4. Click the reset link in the email — it expires after 30 minutes
5. Choose a new password that is at least 12 characters and includes one number and one special character

If you don't receive the reset email, check your spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive after 10 minutes, the email address may not be associated with an active ContosoCloud account.

For security reasons, password reset links can only be used once. If you need a new one, repeat the process above.
