If you believe a charge on your invoice is incorrect, you can open a billing dispute through the ContosoCloud admin console.

## Eligible disputes

You can open a dispute for:

- Charges for services you did not use during the billing period
- Pricing that differs from your contracted rate
- Duplicate charges
- Charges that occurred after a confirmed cancellation

You cannot open a dispute for usage you incurred and verified, even if the cost was higher than expected. For unexpected usage, see the article on cost controls (kb-007).

## How to open a dispute

1. Sign in to console.contosocloud.com
2. Navigate to Billing → Invoices
3. Find the invoice in question and click "View details"
4. Click "Dispute a charge" and select the line items you want to dispute
5. Add a written explanation (required) and attach any supporting documentation
6. Submit. You'll receive an acknowledgement within one business day.

The Billing Operations team reviews disputes within 5 business days. While the dispute is open, the disputed amount is held aside and not subject to late fees. The undisputed portion of your invoice is still due by the original due date.

## Escalation

If your dispute is denied and you believe the denial is incorrect, you can escalate to a human reviewer by replying to the denial email with "Request human review" in the subject line. A senior billing analyst will respond within 3 business days.
